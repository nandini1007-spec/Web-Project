OUR MISSION

Parking should be simple.

PARK-O-BUDDY is an intelligent smart-parking marketplace bridging the gap between frustrated drivers searching for empty spots and property owners with unused driveways and garages.

❌ The Urban Problem

Every single day, urban commuters spend an average of 20 to 25 minutes circling city blocks searching for parking, leading to wasted fuel, massive carbon emissions, and heavy traffic gridlocks.

⏱️ 20+ minutes wasted searching per trip
🚘 Heavy traffic bottlenecks around commercial hubs
💸 High commercial garage rates with hidden surcharges
🚨 Constant risk of municipal towing & wheel-clamping

✅ The PARK-O-BUDDY Solution

A decentralized, verified sharing ecosystem that unlocks existing vacant parking assets: private driveways, residential bays, and underutilized basement slots.
